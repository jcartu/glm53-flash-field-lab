Observed control flow:

1. PrefillComputeShareController.select() receives only two booleans, decode_runnable and prefill_runnable, and chooses a compute service class. It does not receive request length, hot/cold identity, pending decode ancestry, or a per-prefill priority.

2. Waiting requests remain in the configured RequestQueue. With the default FCFS policy, _select_waiting_queue_for_scheduling() returns the queue and schedule() takes request_queue.peek_request(). Compute-share controls whether a prefill-class step runs, but does not reorder the prefills inside that class.

This matches D-Rock's explanation: R25 protects decode versus prefill execution share but does not prioritize or fully interleave queued prefill jobs. The dedicated hot-request field test measures the resulting agent-turn latency and backpressure at C8 and C16.
