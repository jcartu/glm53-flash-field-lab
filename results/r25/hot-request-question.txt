Field-test interpretation:

R25 compute-share fairness protects aggregate execution time between decode and prefill. It does not claim priority or interleaving among queued prefill jobs. The dedicated hot-request benchmark therefore measures cyclic agent turns (decode, small incremental prefill, decode) at C8 and C16 while periodic unique 128K cold prefills arrive. It reports hot-turn TTFT with and without cold work pending, queue depth and area, completed turns, decode gaps, cold drain time, and total useful throughput for fairness off versus compute_share=0.4.
